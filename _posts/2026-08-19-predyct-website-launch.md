The SCAR Krill Expert Group has launched a website for [PREDYCT](https://scarkrillexpertgroup.org/current-work/predyct/) — **Predator Responses to Seasonal Krill Dynamics and Krill Fishing Activities** — the action group coordinating research on how krill-dependent predators respond to changes in krill availability driven by environmental variability and fishing.

The site outlines the programme's structure, including its four working groups, and will serve as a hub for updates as the work progresses. I'm contributing to the programme through my research on krill population connectivity and seasonal dynamics along the Antarctic Peninsula.

Take a look: [scarkrillexpertgroup.org/current-work/predyct](https://scarkrillexpertgroup.org/current-work/predyct/)
