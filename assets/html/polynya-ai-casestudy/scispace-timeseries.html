Key Findings from Time-Series Analysis

📊

Size Difference

The Amundsen Sea Polynya is consistently 2-3× larger than the Pine Island Polynya, with average areas of ~50-80k km² vs ~15-30k km² respectively.

🔄

Seasonal Cycle

Both polynyas show strong seasonal cycles, opening primarily in spring-summer (October-March) and closing during winter months (April-September).

📈

Interannual Trends

2016/17 showed anomalous behavior with record-low sea ice. Pine Island Polynya demonstrates higher year-to-year variability than Amundsen Sea Polynya.

❄️

Ice Production Timing

78% of winter ice production occurs during transition months (April-May and September-October), critical for regional ocean circulation.

Supporting Literature
